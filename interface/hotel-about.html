🏨 About Our Hotel

More than just a place to stay, it's where dreams begin (or where you take a nap)

🌟 Nanxin Grand Hotel - Your Happy Place

Our hotel is located in the center of the universe (okay, actually Nanjing), so conveniently located that even delivery riders can find us after getting lost.

Whether you're here for business, travel, or simply escaping your kids at home, we've prepared cozy and comfortable rooms for you.

Our motto: Make you feel more comfortable than at home (unless you have a private pool and Michelin chef at home).

🍽️ Dining & Cuisine - Foodie Paradise

Our restaurants feature cuisines from around the world, from Nanjing dumplings to French macarons. There's nothing we can't make (except dark cuisine).

Our chefs have worked at Michelin-starred restaurants... well, next door to them, but their skills are definitely Michelin-worthy (self-certified).

Reminder: Please eat responsibly at the buffet. We don't provide "lean on the wall" service.

💼 Meetings & Events - Impress Everyone

Whether it's a company annual dinner, wedding party, or even a "World Staring Contest", we can provide the perfect venue.

Our meeting rooms are equipped with state-of-the-art facilities, including lighting systems that make your boss look better.

Secret: Our projectors are so high-definition, you can see every typo in your PPT.

🏊 Recreation Facilities - Lazy People's Dream

We have a heated swimming pool (warm in winter, cool in summer, perfect for splashing), a gym (with selfie mirrors), and a SPA center (the kind where you can fall asleep).

Work too tired? Try our jacuzzi, it'll make you forget your bank card password (just kidding).

Reminder: No swimming races in the pool, unless you want to challenge the lifeguard.
